Our Story

Built by people who train.

Foxx & Enterprisess started in a small gym locker room, sewing rash guards for training partners who couldn't find gear that actually held up to sparring. Today we outfit fighters, club teams and everyday athletes around the world.

From the mat to the field

Every product line we make traces back to a real need: compression wear that survives a full training camp, uniforms that hold their color through a season of washes, and everyday textile pieces built to be lived in.

We work directly with clubs, gyms and teams to get sizing, fabric and fit right the first time — no guesswork, no returns headache.
